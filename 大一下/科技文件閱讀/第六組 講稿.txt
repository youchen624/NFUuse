// Slide 12 - 解釋關鍵句子
The original line was:
“An API is the messenger that runs and delivers your request to the provider you’re requesting it from, and then delivers the response back to you.”

And in simpler words, I’d say:

“An API is like a middleman — it takes your request, sends it to the system, and brings back the result.”

// Slide 16
One of the harder sentences from the video might be:
“APIs expose data and functionality across the internet in a consistent format.”

This can be a bit tricky to understand, especially if you're new to APIs, because it uses some abstract terms.
For example, what does it mean to “expose data” or “functionality”? And what exactly is a “consistent format”?

Without clear examples, it’s not immediately obvious. So for someone unfamiliar with APIs, this sentence could feel a bit too technical or unclear at first.
